About PRASIT

The story behind the name

Our mission

PRASIT was founded to address a growing problem in Indian academic publishing — the proliferation of predatory conferences that collect fees without providing genuine peer review or credible indexing.

We believe BTech and MTech students who produce original, honest research deserve a platform with real reviewers, meaningful feedback, and verifiable publication records.

PRASIT operates on one principle: quality over volume. We will not compromise peer review standards regardless of submission volume.

We are India-rooted and open to international participation.

प्रसार

Publication & DOI arrangements
What happens to accepted papers

Accepted papers will be published in the PRASIT 2027 Conference Proceedings, an open-access proceedings volume published by PRASIT Research Foundation.

DOI assignment: Each accepted paper will receive a permanent Digital Object Identifier (DOI) issued via CrossRef, the standard DOI registration agency used by IEEE, Springer, Elsevier, and major academic publishers. This DOI makes the paper globally findable and citable. CrossRef membership and DOI issuance is being confirmed; DOIs will only be assigned upon actual registration.

Open access: All proceedings will be freely accessible online at prasit.org. No paywall. No embargo period.

Google Scholar: We will submit the proceedings for indexing in Google Scholar. This is a straightforward process that PRASIT expects to complete successfully, though indexing timelines are set by Google.

Indexing — honest statement: PRASIT 2027 is a first-edition conference. We will submit for Google Scholar indexing. We do not claim Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, or DBLP indexing. Any future indexing in those databases will only be announced after official confirmation. Authors who require Scopus/IEEE-indexed publications for their university requirements should note this before submitting.

Review Process

How peer review works at PRASIT

Transparency about our review process is core to our credibility

The review process
Every paper follows these exact steps
1

Desk check (1 week)

TPC Chair checks: correct format, plagiarism below 15%, within scope, double-blind anonymity maintained. Papers failing desk check are returned immediately with reasons.

2

Reviewer assignment (1 week)

TPC Chair assigns 3 reviewers per paper from the relevant track. Conflict of interest is checked — no reviewer from the same institution as any author.

3

Independent review (4 weeks)

Each reviewer independently evaluates the paper on: originality, technical quality, relevance, writing clarity, and references. Reviewers do not see each other's scores during this phase.

4

TPC discussion (1 week)

For borderline papers, TPC Chair facilitates discussion among reviewers. Final accept/reject decision is based on reviewer consensus and TPC Chair judgment.

5

Author notification (with feedback)

ALL authors — accepted and rejected — receive the full reviewer comments. Rejected authors receive constructive feedback explaining what was lacking and how to improve.

6

Camera-ready & DOI assignment

Accepted authors submit final camera-ready PDF. Each paper is assigned a permanent CrossRef DOI and published in the official PRASIT proceedings.

Our commitment: We target a 25–35% acceptance rate. This is not a quota — it is a quality standard. If submissions are of high quality, acceptance may be higher. If quality is poor, it will be lower.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions we get most

About PRASIT 2027
This is a first-edition conference. Why should I trust it?

That's a fair question to ask of any new conference. We don't have past proceedings to point to yet, so we've tried to make everything else verifiable instead: our full review process is published on the Review Process page, our policies are public, and we're upfront about what's confirmed versus pending (like CrossRef DOI registration). We'd rather under-promise than overstate our status.

Is PRASIT 2027 indexed in Scopus, Web of Science, or IEEE Xplore?

No, not at this time, and we won't claim otherwise. Each accepted paper will receive a CrossRef DOI once registration is finalized, making it citable and findable, and proceedings will be submitted for Google Scholar indexing. Scopus/WoS/IEEE Xplore indexing is a separate, longer process that a first-edition conference is not eligible for immediately.

Is acceptance guaranteed if I pay the registration fee?

No. Every paper goes through double-blind peer review regardless of fee payment, and papers can be rejected. Registration fees cover conference operations (platform, certificates, proceedings assembly) — they are not a pay-to-publish arrangement.

Submission & presentation
Is PRASIT 2027 an online or in-person conference?

Fully online. Authors present live over video call during the 15–16 March 2027 sessions; exact platform and scheduling details will be shared with accepted authors closer to the date.

What if my paper is rejected — do I get any feedback?

Yes. All authors, accepted or rejected, receive the full reviewer comments. Rejected authors get feedback explaining what was lacking and how to improve, rather than a bare rejection notice.

Can I withdraw my paper after it's accepted?

Yes, subject to our refund schedule: full refund if withdrawn more than 30 days before the conference, 50% within 15–30 days, and no refund within 14 days of the conference. See the Policies page for full terms.

Is a fee waiver available?

Students from economically weaker backgrounds may apply for a fee waiver by writing to contact@prasit.org with their institution ID and a brief note.

Policies

Conference policies

PRASIT operates under these policies — applied consistently to all submissions

Publication ethics
PRASIT follows COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) guidelines

Authorship: All listed authors must have made a genuine intellectual contribution to the work. Honorary, gift, or ghost authorship is not permitted. The corresponding author is responsible for ensuring all co-authors have approved the final submission and agree to its publication.

Originality: Submitted work must be original and must not be under review at any other conference or journal simultaneously. Duplicate submission — submitting the same paper to two venues at the same time — will result in immediate rejection from PRASIT and notification to the other venue if identified.

Data integrity: Fabrication, falsification, or selective reporting of data is a serious breach of ethics. Authors must be able to provide underlying data upon request. PRASIT reserves the right to retract published papers if post-publication misconduct is established.

AI-assisted writing: Use of generative AI tools for writing assistance is permitted but must be disclosed in the paper. AI tools may not be listed as authors. The human authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy and integrity of the content.

Plagiarism policy
Checked at desk review before any paper enters peer review
✅

Acceptable: below 15%

Papers with similarity below 15% (excluding references and template boilerplate) proceed to peer review.

⚠️

Borderline: 15–25%

Papers in this range are reviewed by the TPC Chair. If similarity is concentrated in one source, the paper may be desk-rejected or returned for revision.

❌

Rejected: above 25%

Papers above 25% similarity are rejected at desk check. The submitting author is notified with the similarity report.

🚫

Self-plagiarism

Submitting your own prior work without disclosure and proper citation is treated as plagiarism and results in desk rejection.

PRASIT uses iThenticate or a comparable academic plagiarism detection tool for all submissions. The similarity report result does not override human judgment — a paper with 16% similarity from properly cited sources may pass; a paper with 12% similarity copied verbatim from one source may not.
Conflict of interest (COI) policy
Applied to all reviewers and TPC members

A conflict of interest exists when a reviewer has a professional or personal relationship with an author that could affect their objectivity. The following situations constitute a COI and require a reviewer to recuse themselves:

Same institution

The reviewer is currently employed at or enrolled in the same university or organization as any author.

Recent co-authorship

The reviewer has co-authored a paper with any author in the last 3 years.

Advisor / advisee relationship

The reviewer has served (or currently serves) as the PhD/MTech advisor or supervisee of any author.

Personal or financial conflict

The reviewer has a close personal relationship with, or financial interest in, any author's work.

Authors may declare COIs at submission time by listing names of individuals they believe should not review their paper, with a brief reason. Such requests are considered by the TPC Chair but are not guaranteed to be honored.
Privacy policy
What data PRASIT collects and how it is used

Data collected: When you submit a paper, register, or contact us, we collect your name, email address, institutional affiliation, and any information you voluntarily provide. Paper content is collected for the purpose of review and, if accepted, publication.

How it is used: Your contact information is used solely for conference operations — review assignments, acceptance notifications, registration confirmation, and proceedings publication. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Submission portal: Paper submissions, registration, and review are handled through the PRASIT Submission Portal (prasit-portal.vercel.app), operated directly by PRASIT Research Foundation. Account and submission data entered on the portal is stored and processed by PRASIT for the purposes described above.

Proceedings: Accepted paper authors consent to having their name, affiliation, paper title, and abstract published in the PRASIT 2027 proceedings and indexed publicly (Google Scholar, CrossRef).

Retention: Submission and review data is retained for a minimum of 5 years for academic integrity purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data (excluding published proceedings) by writing to contact@prasit.org.

Withdrawal & refund policy
Read before paying registration

Paper withdrawal before review: Authors may withdraw a submitted paper at any time before the review assignment is complete by writing to contact@prasit.org. No fee applies at this stage since registration is paid after acceptance.

Withdrawal after acceptance: If an accepted author withdraws after paying registration, the paper will be removed from the proceedings. Refund eligibility: full refund if withdrawn more than 30 days before the conference date; 50% refund within 15–30 days; no refund within 14 days of the conference.

Conference cancellation: In the event PRASIT 2027 is cancelled for reasons outside our control, registered participants will receive a full refund within 30 working days.

All policy queries should be directed to contact@prasit.org. Policies may be updated; the version published on prasit.org at the time of submission applies.
